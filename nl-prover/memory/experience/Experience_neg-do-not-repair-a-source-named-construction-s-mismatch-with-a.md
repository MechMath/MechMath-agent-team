statement: Do not repair a source-named construction's mismatch with a target's fixed scale-invariant origin ratio by adding post hoc support-intercept, parameter, reweighting, or gauge boxes.
trigger: A structurally plausible source construction has its normalization fixed before comparison, but its intrinsic origin ratio disagrees exactly with the target initial ratio.
why: A common scale cannot change the ratio, and fitting extra choices after observing the initial data manufactures an unbounded search for a different selected solution rather than repairing the declared construction.
failure_modes: Do not over-apply this boundary when a different moving path, parameter assignment, or gauge is intrinsic to a separately named construction or forced by an independently derived target equation; such a route may be audited as a new contract.
