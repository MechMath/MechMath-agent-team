# Summary Outputs

Automatic and manual summarize use the `proof-summarize` skill at
`.agents/skills/proof-summarize/SKILL.md`.

Outputs may include:

- `../data/inbox/<problem_id>_correct.md`
- `../data/inbox/<problem_id>_errors.md`
- repository-root `memory.md`
- `../data/workspace/<problem_id>/proof_summary.tex`

`memory.md` is a compact recurring-error memory. Keep it English-only,
deduplicated, and at 100 lines or fewer. Do not write directly to KB-Manager
during summarize.

Do not confuse repository-root `memory.md` with workspace-local
`memory/*.jsonl`. The JSONL files are mechanical per-workspace indexes written by
the local tier of `memory.py` (`memory.py refresh`); they summarize branch state,
failed paths, verification reports, source findings, and presentation artifacts
for later agents. They are not proof evidence and do not replace `proof.tex`,
Verifier packets, query outputs, or `memory.md`. Note `memory.md` is the resident
long-term negative-constraint list, generated by `memory.py render-longterm` from
the repo-local `Experience_*` cards in `memory/experience/` — read it, do not
hand-edit it.
